# Datasets

## Items written for kasauti

| Task | Source | License | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| All v1 tasks | Written by kasauti contributors, each item reviewed by at least two other people | CC-BY-4.0 | `src/kasauti/tasks/data/` |

No item is copied from exams, books, websites or other datasets.

**How items are made.** Some first drafts were written with help from an AI assistant (Claude). Every item is then edited and fact-checked by its human author and approved by at least two other human reviewers before it ships. Items waiting for review live in `datasets/pending/` and are never scored. See [datasets/README.md](datasets/README.md) for the review process.

## External datasets

None yet. Any external dataset added later must have an open license (CC-BY, CC0, Apache-2.0 or MIT) and will be listed here with its license and a link. Data under non-commercial or unclear licenses is never stored in this repository; if supported at all, it is downloaded at runtime with clear attribution.
